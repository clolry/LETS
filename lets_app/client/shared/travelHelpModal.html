Step 1: Trip Overview

Enter the event or meeting name, select a location type (Client Site, Conference, Contractor Site, TEB, or Other), and pick the event date range. Then provide a one-sentence BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) and a Detailed Purpose & Justification explaining why this travel is necessary.

Step 2: Itinerary

Build your trip itinerary by adding each destination stop in order. For each stop you'll enter:

  • Travel Start & End Dates — The first day of travel to that destination through the last day. For multi-stop trips, the next destination's start date can be the same as the previous destination's end date.
  • Country, State & City — Start typing to search. City suggestions include locations with special per diem rates.
  • Site Name — Only appears when the location type is Client Site or Contractor Site (set in Step 1). Enter the name of the specific site you're visiting.

Click Add Another Stop for multi-city trips. The form builds a route summary as you go.

Tip: If you select an international destination, a notice will appear letting you know that additional information will be collected in the Travelers step.

Step 3: Travelers

Add each traveler on the trip. The form progressively reveals fields as you go:

  1. Employee — Search by name using the typeahead. Once selected, the form pulls in their role, duty location, and BU automatically.
  2. Role Justification — Explain why this specific traveler needs to go on the trip.
  3. Funding — Select Overhead (agency-funded) or Client (client-paid).
  4. Primary Travel Method — Choose Air, Rail, Rental Car, POV (private vehicle), or GOV (government vehicle). Enter the estimated cost or total miles depending on the mode.
  5. Per Diem — Lodging and M&IE costs are calculated automatically per destination based on GSA rates. You can override any amount if needed (e.g., conference hotel rate).

Local Travel

If a destination is local (no overnight stay), there is no lodging or M&IE. Instead, you'll enter commute information (normal round-trip miles and daily parking), then estimate miles driven, excess parking, and tolls for each local leg. Only costs above your normal commute are reimbursable.

International Travel

If the itinerary includes an international destination, an additional section appears for each traveler attending that leg. You'll be asked to confirm:

  • Valid passport (not expiring within 6 months)
  • Security clearance status
  • Whether you're speaking or presenting
  • Whether the event is open to press/media
  • Whether receiving non-federal source (NFS) funding or gifts — if yes, you'll need to download and upload the NFS Worksheet

A running total at the bottom of the step shows cost breakdowns (transport, lodging, M&IE, local, 15% gross-up) across all travelers.

Step 4: Travel Classification

Two checkbox columns — select all that apply in each:

  • Type of Travel — Local, Internal, Customer Meeting, Vendor Meeting, International, Overseas Employee, Speaking Engagement, GSA Conference, Non-GSA Conference/Training.
  • Mission-Critical Travel — SCIF activities, physical oversight, vehicle inspections, retail operations, industry engagement, or Technical Evaluation Boards.

Step 5: Shared Costs (Optional)

If there are costs shared across travelers, enter them in four categories: Facilities & Space, Audio/Visual & Tech, Logistics & Operations, and Other. Each category has an amount field and an optional description.

Step 6: Review & Submit

Upload any supporting documents (drag & drop, browse files, or select from Google Drive — PDFs and images up to 10MB). Review all the information you've entered across every step. The form shows a grand total combining traveler costs and shared costs. When everything looks good, click Submit Request.

Tip: You can save a draft at any time and come back later. See the Working with Drafts section for details.

The Approval Flow

Every travel request moves through a series of review stages. The exact path depends on whether the travel is overhead-funded or client-paid.

Standard Flow

Submitted Sector Review BU Review AAS FO Review FAS Review* Approved DD Confirm Complete

*FAS Review only applies to overhead-funded travel.

What Happens at Each Stage

  • Sector Review — Your sector leadership reviews the request and confirms it aligns with organizational priorities.
  • BU Review — Your Business Unit reviewer checks budget availability and travel details.
  • AAS FO Review — The AAS Front Office reviews and verifies the request.
  • FAS Review — Only for overhead-funded travel. FAS leadership makes the final budget authorization.
  • Approved — The request is authorized. You'll receive an email confirmation with booking instructions.
  • DD Confirmation — Division Directors confirm the travelers on the approved trip and flag any funding concerns.
  • Complete — All confirmations are done. The trip is fully authorized and recorded.

Overhead vs. Client-Paid Path

If all travelers on the request are client-paid, the request skips FAS Review and moves directly from AAS FO Review to Approved status. If there's a mix of overhead and client-paid travelers, the request goes through FAS Review for the overhead portion.

Tip: At any review stage, the reviewer may send the request back to you for more information. Check the When Changes Are Needed section for details on that process.

Viewing Request Details

Click View on any request card to open the review page. There you'll see all the trip details, the full approval timeline, and any feedback from reviewers.

When Changes Are Needed

If a reviewer needs more information or finds an issue, they'll send the request back to you with a "Needs Info" status and a note explaining what's needed.

How to Update & Resubmit

  1. Look for the request in your Action Required section — it will have the card shown above.
  2. Click Update to open the request for editing.
  3. Read the reviewer's feedback at the top of the form.
  4. Make the requested changes to any step.
  5. Click Update & Resubmit to send it back into the approval flow.

The request will return to the same review stage it was at when the reviewer sent it back.

Funding Corrections

If a Division Director flags a funding issue during DD Confirmation, you'll see a Funding Correction Required action item on your portal. Click it to open the correction form, update the funding type for the flagged travelers, and resubmit.

Tip: You'll receive an email notification whenever a request is returned for changes. Check your inbox if you're not sure what needs updating.

Cancelling a Request

Who Can Cancel

  • You (the submitter) can cancel your own request at any time before it reaches "Completed" status.
  • Reviewers can cancel a request from the review page.
  • Admins can cancel any request from the Admin Console.

How to Cancel

Click View on the request to open the review page. In the sidebar, click the dropdown arrow next to the action button and select Cancel Request. You'll need to provide a reason. Once confirmed, the request is permanently moved to "Cancelled" status and no further reviews occur.

What Happens After

Cancelled requests remain visible in your My Requests list with a "Cancelled" badge. They cannot be re-opened. If you still need the travel authorized, submit a new request.

Working with Drafts

Drafts let you start a request and come back to it later without losing any data.

Saving a Draft

While filling out the request form, click Save Draft at any point. Your progress is saved locally in your browser. You'll see a confirmation message when the draft is saved.

Resuming a Draft

Saved drafts appear in the left sidebar of the portal. Click a draft to re-open the form exactly where you left off. All fields will be pre-filled with your saved data.

Deleting a Draft

Hover over a draft in the sidebar and click the trash icon to delete it. Drafts are also automatically removed when you successfully submit the request.

Tip: Drafts are stored in your browser's local storage. If you switch to a different browser or clear your browser data, your drafts won't carry over.

Request More Information

If something is unclear or missing, click the dropdown arrow and select Request More Information. Your section comments are bundled into the notification. You can also add an overall message. The request is sent back to the submitter with "Needs Info" status.

Denying a Request

Click the dropdown arrow and select Deny Request. You must provide a reason. The submitter will be notified by email.

Cancelling from the Review Page

Both reviewers and submitters can cancel a request from the review page. Click the dropdown arrow and select Cancel Request. You'll need to provide a reason. This permanently cancels the request.

DD Confirmation

After a request is fully approved, Division Directors receive an email for each traveler in their division asking them to confirm the travel.

What Division Directors Do

  • Review the traveler details and funding type in the email.
  • Confirm the traveler to acknowledge they're authorized to travel.
  • Flag funding issues if a traveler's funding type appears incorrect (e.g., marked as overhead but should be client-paid).

How It Works

DD Confirmations arrive via email with a link to the confirmation page. Click the link to see the traveler's details and funding type, then confirm or flag the issue. All DDs for a request must confirm before it reaches "Completed" status.

Flagging Issues

If you flag a funding issue, the request is sent back to the submitter for a Funding Correction. Add a note explaining the issue so the submitter knows what to fix.

Tip: Check your email for DD Confirmation requests. They include a direct link to the confirmation page — no need to navigate through the portal.

FAS Decisions

FAS leadership makes the final budget authorization decision for overhead-funded travel. This step only applies to requests with at least one overhead-funded traveler.

Approving from the Portal

FAS decisions can be made directly from the portal. When a request reaches FAS Review, it appears in your Action Required section. Click FAS Decision to open this compact modal:

Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard gives you a real-time picture of the entire travel request pipeline.

Action Center

The top section shows two cards:

  • Your Reviews — Requests currently assigned to you for review, with quick links to each one.
  • Stalled Requests — Requests that have been sitting at a review stage for an unusually long time. Use this to follow up with reviewers.

Pipeline

The pipeline bar shows how many requests are at each stage of the approval flow. Click any stage count to jump to a filtered view in the Requests tab.

Stat Cards

Four key metrics displayed prominently:

  • Avg. Approval Time — Average days from submission to final approval.
  • Avg. Lead Time — Average days between submission and the event start date.
  • Denied — Number of requests denied in the selected period.
  • Cancelled — Number of requests cancelled in the selected period.

Charts

Two visualizations help spot trends:

  • Approval Time by Stage — Bar chart showing average review time at each stage.
  • Spend by BU — Bar chart showing estimated travel spend broken down by Business Unit.

Reviewer Performance Table

At the bottom of the dashboard, a table shows each active reviewer with their average review time, number of reviews completed, and current pending count. Sector reviewers are collapsed by default — click to expand.

Tip: Use the period selector at the top of the dashboard to change the reporting window (e.g., Last 30 Days, Last Quarter). Pending counts and volume trends are always shown as current totals regardless of the selected period.

Managing Requests

The Requests tab provides a full table of every travel request in the system with powerful filtering and sorting.

Filtering & Sorting

  • Status filter — Filter by any status (Pending, Approved, Denied, etc.) or show all.
  • BU filter — Narrow to requests from a specific Business Unit.
  • Search — Type to search by submitter name, event name, or request ID.
  • Column sorting — Click any column header to sort ascending/descending.

Slide-In Detail Panel

Click any row to open a slide-in panel on the right side showing the full request details:

  • Trip information and traveler list
  • Complete approval timeline with reviewer notes
  • Current status and stage
  • View Full Review Page link to open the complete review page for deeper inspection

Admin Actions

From the detail panel, admins can:

  • Override Status — Change a request's status (e.g., move a stuck request forward, or revert a status). Use sparingly and document your reason.
  • Reassign Reviewer — Move a request to a different reviewer at the current stage.
  • Cancel Request — Cancel any active request.
Tip: All admin actions are logged in the approval timeline, so there's always a record of what happened and who did it.

Managing Reviewers

The Reviewers tab has two sub-tabs: Reviewers (manages who reviews at each stage) and Admin Access (manages who can access the admin console).

AAS FO Reviewers & Delegation

AAS Front Office reviewers are displayed as cards grouped by role. The Delegation toggle at the top lets you switch between the primary AAS FO reviewer and a delegate. When delegation is active, the delegate receives review assignments instead of the primary reviewer.

BU Reviewers

BU reviewers are listed below the AAS FO section. Each BU reviewer is assigned to a specific Business Unit.

  • Add — Click "Add BU Reviewer" to open the form. Search for an employee using the typeahead, select their BU, and save.
  • Edit — Click the edit icon on a BU reviewer card. Change the assigned email, and the system will offer to reassign any pending requests.
  • Delete — Click the delete icon. If the reviewer has pending requests, you'll be prompted to reassign them first.

Admin Access

Switch to the Admin Access sub-tab to manage who has access to the admin console. Admins can view all requests, override statuses, manage reviewers, and run reports.

Tip: Changes to BU reviewer assignments take effect immediately. New admins may need to refresh the page to see the admin console link on the portal.
